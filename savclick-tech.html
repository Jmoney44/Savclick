What we offer

Four ways we can help, depending on how deep the problem goes.

MacBook and iMac hardware repair

Screens, batteries, keyboards, trackpads or mouse-related faults, speakers and charging ports, replaced or repaired.

Advanced logic board repair

When a part swap won't fix it, we work directly on the board, diagnosing and replacing faulty ICs and board-level components where repair is possible.

Software and system repair

Compatible macOS installation, system troubleshooting, Wi-Fi and network issues caused by software or configuration, and system setup.

Professional consultation

We inspect the problem, explain what may be causing it, and advise you on the right next step.

Hardware repair

Physical faults, diagnosed first and then repaired with the appropriate compatible parts.

Screen replacement

Cracked, damaged, flickering or faulty displays replaced with the appropriate compatible screen.

Battery replacement

Not holding charge, shutting down unexpectedly, or showing a service warning? We diagnose it and replace the battery where necessary.

Keyboard and trackpad

Individual keys, full keyboard assemblies and trackpad or mouse-related faults, repaired or replaced.

Speaker repair

Low, distorted or silent speakers are checked and repaired or replaced where required.

Charging port

A charging problem can come from the port, the board, the cable path or the power circuit. We find which before we repair.

Advanced logic board repair

When the problem is deeper than a replaceable part, we work directly on the MacBook logic board instead of simply swapping the whole thing.

Chip-level work

We troubleshoot faults in integrated circuits and other board-level components.

Professional equipment

Microscopes, soldering equipment, hot-air and rework tools, precision workstations and proper lighting.

Best practices

Careful inspection, controlled soldering and rework, then testing before the machine goes back to you.

Fault diagnosis

Power, charging, boot, display, USB and Thunderbolt faults are investigated until we locate the actual problem.

Software and system repair

Not every fault is hardware. We handle the software side too.

macOS installation

A compatible macOS version for your MacBook or iMac, based on the model and what it supports.

Network issues

Wi-Fi, network configuration and connectivity problems where the cause is software or configuration.

System troubleshooting

Boot problems, system errors and configuration issues, assessed and resolved.

Setup and optimisation

A clean system setup and the essential configuration to get your Mac ready for normal use.

Why choose us

Our goal is not simply to make a Mac power on. It's to find the actual fault, repair it properly and test the device before it comes back to you.

Authenticity and quality

Appropriate, reliable replacement parts and proper repair procedures, with no shortcuts.

Technical expertise

From everyday Mac hardware problems to advanced board-level faults, including chip-level work.

Proper equipment

Microscopes, precision soldering irons, rework equipment, workstations and proper lighting let us inspect and work on delicate components accurately.

Best-practice workmanship

Inspect the fault, diagnose the cause, carry out the repair, then test the machine before return.

Clear communication

We explain the problem we found and the repair we recommend, so you know what's being done to your Mac.

Reliable service

Every repair is tested properly before we hand the device back.
