Ways to learn

Pick the format that fits your time, budget and goals.

Group classes

Learn alongside other students on a set schedule. A good way to start, with classmates to practise with.

Private one-on-one

Training for just you, at times that suit you, going at your own pace and focused on what you need.

Team and corporate training

Training for your staff, built around your business, at our space or yours.

Why learn with us

Our trainers aren't only teachers. They repair Macs, build websites and run campaigns for SAVCLICK clients every week.

Taught by practitioners

You learn from people who do this work for real clients, not only from slides.

Hands-on from day one

Most of your time is spent practising on real tools, devices and projects.

Proper equipment

Repair students train with the same microscopes, soldering and rework tools used in our workshop.

Small classes

Fewer students per class, so every student gets attention and their questions answered.
