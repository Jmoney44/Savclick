What we automate

Start with one room or one system, or automate the whole building. Every device can be controlled from your phone, on a schedule, or by voice.

Smart lighting

Smart switches, dimmers and bulbs. Turn lights on and off from anywhere, set schedules and create scenes for every mood.

Smart locks and access

Fingerprint, PIN, card and phone-controlled locks. Give and remove access for family, staff or guests in seconds.

Security cameras and doorbells

Indoor and outdoor cameras with night vision, motion alerts and video doorbells you can answer from your phone.

Curtains and blinds

Motorised curtains and blinds that open with the morning and close at night, on their own or at a tap.

Climate and AC control

Control air conditioners from your phone, set temperatures and schedules, and stop paying to cool empty rooms.

Gates and garages

Open and close your gate or garage from your phone, and share access with the people who need it.

Sensors and alerts

Motion, door, window, smoke and water-leak sensors that warn you the moment something happens.

App and voice control

Everything in one app, with scenes and automations. Works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant.

For homes and for hotels

The same technology, set up around how you actually use the space.

Homes and offices

Comfort, safety and control

  • Lights, AC and curtains that follow your daily routine
  • See who is at the gate or door from anywhere
  • Smart locks, so no more lost or copied keys
  • "Away" mode that turns everything off when you leave
  • Alerts for motion, smoke or water leaks
Hotels and short-lets

Better guest experience, lower running costs

  • RFID card and PIN door locks with a management system
  • Key-card power saving: lights and AC switch off when guests leave
  • Remote check-in codes for short-let apartments
  • Welcome scenes with lights and curtains ready on arrival
  • CCTV for corridors, entrances and car parks
  • Digital Kitchen menu, DND Switch and Room Signage for guest convenience
