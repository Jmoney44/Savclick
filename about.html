About SAVCLICK

Five businesses.
One name behind all of them.

SAVCLICK Limited is a Nigerian holding company. We don't run one business wearing five different signs — we run five real businesses, each with its own team and focus, all held to the same standard and backed by the same name.

Our story

Built one department at a time

SAVCLICK started as a single business solving a single problem. As the work grew, it became clear that trying to force every new idea into one website, one team, and one brand voice was holding each idea back rather than helping it.

So SAVCLICK became a holding company — a building, not a shop. Every new business gets its own space inside it: its own logo, its own site, its own way of talking to its own customers. What stays the same, floor to floor, is the name on the front door and the standard of work behind it.

Today that building has five addresses — SAVCLICK Tech, Digitex, Academy, Travels, and Properties — with Tech, Digitex, and Academy already open for business.

Our mission

Give every good idea its own proper business

To build and run independent, well-run businesses under one trusted name — instead of stretching one brand thin across everything it touches.

Our vision

One address Nigerians recognize and trust

To make SAVCLICK a name people associate with dependable service, wherever they meet it — in a repair shop, a training room, a booking, or a storefront.

What holds every department together
