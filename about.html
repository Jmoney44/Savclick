Who We Are

The name carries the fix.

Sav is the discipline: stripping unnecessary time, cost, and second-guessing out of every interaction we design. Click is the promise: once a client knows what they want, acting on it should take exactly one confident step. Put together, Savclick is not just a name, it is a standard we hold ourselves to.

What we do

We are deliberately not a single-product company chasing a single market. Today, Savclick operates across creative and digital services, technology integration, smart home automation, computer engineering, and the trading of goods and services — because complexity shows up everywhere, and we intend to be the company clients turn to whenever they are in need of excellent service and product delivery. Our task is not to sell technology for its own sake. It is to hand people and businesses back their time, their certainty, and their control over the choices in front of them.

Our story

Two friends who refused to let an idea go

Savclick began on 1 January 2019, the way many enduring companies do: not as a funded venture, but as an idea two university friends in Nigeria refused to let go of. Shadrach Ifeanyi Mbah and Victor Nwajei Aibangbe built the earliest version of Savclick around the two things they had in surplus and the market had in short supply, genuine creative skill, and the discipline to actually finish what they promised a client.

The name itself is a small monument to that partnership. SAV was never only shorthand for "save", it stood, from the beginning, for Shadrach And Victor, compressed into a mark simple enough to fit on a business card and confident enough to mean something larger later. CLICK, characteristically, was the promise attached from day one: that working with them should feel immediate, never laborious.

For its first two years, the company earned its name before it had any paperwork behind it, through advertisement animation and storytelling for businesses, graphics design, digital marketing, web development, and video editing.

  1. 2019

    Founded

    Shadrach Ifeanyi Mbah and Victor Nwajei Aibangbe start building Savclick around creative work and a promise to actually finish what they said they would.

  2. 2021

    Registered as SAVCLICK DIGITEX

    Two years of advertisement animation, graphic design, digital marketing, web development and video editing are formalized with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission.

  3. 2024

    In memory of a co-founder

    Savclick loses one of its founders. Engr. Shadrach Ifeanyi Mbah, the S in SAV, and half of the instinct that built this company, passes away, leaving behind a body of work and a standard the organisation continues to build against.

  4. 2026

    Incorporated as SAVCLICK LIMITED

    Savclick becomes a private limited liability company. Incorporation widens what the company does: beyond its original creative and digital services, Savclick now provides trading of goods and services alongside two new technical departments, Smart Home Automation and Computer Engineering.

Every value, and every client interaction carried out under this name, still answers to the bar Shadrach helped set. The company Victor now leads carries more disciplines than it did in 2019, but it is still measured by the same question the two friends asked at the start: "Does this actually make things easier for the person on the other end of it?"

What holds every department together

Our core values

Client Centricity

Every product, workflow, and service is designed around the client's actual need, not around what is easiest for us to build. Value and ease of use are never traded off against each other here, both are non-negotiable.

Technological Accessibility

We do the hard technical work internally so the client never has to. Sophisticated capability should arrive at the client's end looking effortless, because the complexity was ours to absorb, not theirs to navigate.

Operational Excellence

We enforce strict quality standards across all our business units, because trust is built through countlessly small, flawless details, and it can be lost the moment a single one fails.
